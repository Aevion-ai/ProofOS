Self-improving agents may rewrite their context, memory, skills, and
harness, but they cannot rewrite the authority conditions under which
their changes become real. We have presented a fail-closed architecture in
which every consequential transition discharges typed obligations for
identity, authority, evidence, program and environment binding,
continuity, and effect scope; reflection is separated from promotion;
receipts bind replay and resume semantics; and independent authority roots
are required for quorum.

The reference implementation and its evaluation battery demonstrate the
gate's behavior on adversarial fixtures, including the distinction between
admission disposition and execution outcome. The released artifact is an
open research extraction: source, schemas, formal statements, tests, and
explicit claim ceilings, reproducible locally and in CI.

Future work proceeds along three lanes: (i) live integration with a
long-horizon agent harness and middle-layer activation capture; (ii)
verified linking between the Lean statements and the Python gate; and
(iii) deployment of the gate at the tool-invocation boundary alongside
centralized write-control layers such as WriteGuard, with authority and
receipt validation layered above.
