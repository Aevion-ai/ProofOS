A receipt must bind the components of a transition:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\texttt{execution\_graph\_digest} & the compiled graph the action derived from \\
\texttt{xal\_program\_digest} & the assembly-like execution program \\
\texttt{authority\_lease} & the lease that authorized the transition \\
\texttt{environment\_digest} & the execution environment binding \\
\texttt{input\_digest} & the transition input \\
\texttt{effects\_digest} & the declared side effects \\
\texttt{result\_digest} & the execution result \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{theorem}[Receipt divergence invalidates]\label{thm:divergence}
Digest divergence between a signed receipt and the current bindings
invalidates replay or promotion.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
The receipt body hash is computed over the canonical serialization of all
seven bindings plus the transition origin. Any divergence between the
recorded bindings and the current bindings changes the recomputed hash,
so verification fails before replay or promotion is attempted.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Consumed resume cannot fire twice]\label{thm:resume}
A consumed resume must not fire twice.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Each resume carries a unique nonce and an effect idempotency key. The
resume gate rejects when the nonce or the effect key already appears in
the consumed set. The Lean statement
\texttt{consumed\_resume\_cannot\_fire\_twice} and the executable
\texttt{resume\_cannot\_repeat\_consumed\_effect} both enforce this.
\end{proof}

\begin{theorem}[Invalid checkpoint cannot resume]\label{thm:checkpoint}
A resume referencing a checkpoint whose state digest does not match the
recorded state is invalid.
\end{theorem}

Beyond admission disposition, a receipt records the transition origin and
the execution outcome. A command may be authorized but fail at execution;
the receipt must record both independently:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\texttt{admission\_disposition} & ADMIT $\mid$ REJECT \\
\texttt{execution\_outcome} & SUCCESS $\mid$ FAILURE $\mid$ TIMEOUT $\mid$ PLATFORM\_ERROR \\
\texttt{origin} & NEW $\mid$ RETRIED $\mid$ RESUMED $\mid$ FORKED $\mid$ REPLAYED $\mid$ COMPENSATED \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Authorization is not a claim that execution succeeded. An ADMIT receipt
with \texttt{execution\_outcome=FAILURE} is the honest record of an
authorized command that failed.
