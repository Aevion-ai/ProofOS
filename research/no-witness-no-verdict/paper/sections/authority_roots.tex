A second agent account does not constitute an independent reviewer when
both identities derive from the same authority, credential, model-session,
or campaign root.

\begin{theorem}[Same root is not quorum]\label{thm:root}
Two identities derived from the same authority root do not constitute an
independent endorsement.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
Independence is defined over the authority root, not over the agent
identifier. The Lean statement
\texttt{same\_authority\_root\_is\_not\_quorum} and the executable
\texttt{same\_authority\_root\_is\_not\_independent} both return false when
the roots are equal.
\end{proof}

This rule has operational force in review and promotion paths: a second
account created under the same campaign credential, a model session
replayed under the same lease, or a sub-agent spawned by the same principal
all share a root. Their endorsements are correlated, not independent, and
cannot discharge quorum obligations. The evaluation battery includes the
same-root review fixture (expected HOLD or REJECT).
